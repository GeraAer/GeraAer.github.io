Controls & project notes

Controls

WASD or arrow keys to move. E to interact. Use the illustrated buttons to take orders, cook, buy supplies and open the journal. Tab switches rooms. Press Esc and choose Save to keep your café progress in the browser.

Project notes

A game in development about running a coffeehouse for witches. Cook orders, develop recipes, improve the shop and follow the Lantern Tea Party chapter.

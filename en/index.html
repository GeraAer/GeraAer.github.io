Background & influences

I’m Mayflare, a member of Mayflare Magic studio — yuri fan, game developer, and sci-fi writer.

I received offers / admission from Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) ETC and Duke GDDI, and ultimately chose the HKUST Guangzhou (HKUST GZ) MFA.

I read widely across games, film, and literature — especially K.J. Parker’s cool, precise, darkly funny craft.
I love designing emergence: simple rules that collide into surprising behavior, as in Throng: Evolution.

I build with Unity and Ren’Py. Long-running projects include Lastrip (a Mayflare Magic post-apocalyptic yuri VN) and the online sandbox Xenia Kingdom.
Ruri Empress is an epistolary yuri visual novel available on itch.io. The full HTML game is playable in your browser.

A relationship through sparring

DuaLily explores companionship with Akane through everyday life and practice. Read an incoming move, parry or dodge, then counter when an opening appears.

Development context

The illustrated room comes from the earlier interface prototype. The video shows the separate Unity combat prototype: the pieces are still in development. It is not a finished release or a public browser demo.

The combat capture is an existing native scene recording with its recorded audio. The prototype includes imported third-party models, animations, effects and audio alongside project code and visual experiments; it is not a claim that every asset was made by the creator.
